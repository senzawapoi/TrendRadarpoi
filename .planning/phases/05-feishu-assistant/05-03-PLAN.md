<objective>
Make Feishu authorization, Assistant session association, event deduplication, and reconnect behavior
safe across duplicate delivery, process restart, and connection failure.

Purpose: ensure retries never create duplicate Gemini calls or turns, unauthorized events have no
side effects, and a one-CPU VPS cannot enter an unbounded reconnect storm.
</objective>
