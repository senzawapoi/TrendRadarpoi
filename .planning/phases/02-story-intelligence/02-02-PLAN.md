<objective>
Migrate only intelligence.db from schema v1 to v2 and provide an idempotent Story persistence
service that keeps Story IDs and memberships stable as new canonical items arrive.
</objective>

<tasks>

<task type="implementation">
  <name>Add a forward-only v2 schema migration</name>
  <files>trendradar/intelligence/storage.py</files>
  <action>
    Replace the single schema-version assignment with explicit migration steps. Preserve all v1
    tables and data, create versioned clusters, stories, and story_members tables with foreign keys,
    indexes, unique story identity and unique (story_id, content_item_id) membership constraints,
    then set PRAGMA user_version=2 in one transaction. Reject unsupported future versions. Keep WAL,
    foreign keys, busy timeout, and existing transaction behavior. Keep SQL and row conversion inside
    IntelligenceStorage and expose typed get/list/upsert methods for Story and membership records.
  </action>
  <done>Fresh and v1 intelligence databases expose schema v2, preserve Phase 1 rows, and retain WAL, foreign keys, timeout, and atomic rollback behavior.</done>
  <verify>python -m pytest tests/intelligence/test_storage.py -q</verify>
  <acceptance_criteria>Fresh and v1 databases open at version 2; v1 content/observations survive;
    migration is repeatable; foreign keys and atomic rollback remain enabled.</acceptance_criteria>
</task>

<task type="implementation">
  <name>Persist stable incremental Story membership</name>
  <files>trendradar/intelligence/stories.py, trendradar/intelligence/storage.py</files>
  <action>
    Add a StoryService/derive_stories boundary accepting canonical items and optional existing
    stories. Process items and clusters in stable order. Resolve a new cluster to exactly one
    existing Story by deterministic member overlap/signature within domain and time rules; retain
    that ID and append unique members. Create a deterministic singleton or new Story otherwise;
    leave ambiguous matches separate. Recompute derived display fields without replacing IDs.
    Write clusters, stories, and all memberships in one short storage transaction after any
    generation work. Return canonical Story objects and counts; make repeated batches no-ops.
  </action>
  <done>StoryService preserves IDs across compatible incremental arrivals, isolates ambiguous matches, and reports zero duplicate memberships on replay.</done>
  <verify>python -m pytest tests/intelligence/test_stories.py -q</verify>
  <acceptance_criteria>Reversed input and repeated execution produce identical Story IDs and one
    membership per pair; later source arrival extends the original Story; all canonical members
    and observation provenance remain queryable.</acceptance_criteria>
</task>
